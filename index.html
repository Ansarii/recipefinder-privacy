1. Information We Collect

Recipe Finder collects and processes the following categories of information to provide and improve our services:

a. User Information

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Profile details (if applicable)
  • Favorite recipes, cuisines, or dietary preferences
  • Search queries and recipe history

b. Device Information

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and unique device identifiers
  • Mobile network information (for app functionality)

c. Usage Data

  • Features used
  • Time spent within the app
  • Error logs and crash reports

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: To provide recipe search, filtering, and personalized content.
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, and improve performance.
  • Technical Support: To diagnose technical issues and respond to user inquiries.
  • Personalization: To tailor recipe recommendations and search results.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party services assisting us in app operations (e.g., analytics, recipe APIs).
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or government request.
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights, property, or safety, and that of users.
  • Business Transfers: In case of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Third-Party Services

Recipe Finder integrates third-party APIs and analytics tools that may collect and process limited data:

a. Recipe Data APIs

  • TheMealDB – provides global recipe data and images
  • CocktailDB – supplies drink and cocktail recipes
  • DummyJSON – backup recipe data source

b. Analytics and Crash Reporting

  • Flutter Analytics – monitors performance and crash logs
  • Local Analytics – anonymized usage data stored locally

For detailed data practices, please refer to each service provider’s privacy policy.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS)
  • Secure local data storage
  • Regular updates and security assessments

While we use industry-standard safeguards, no method of data transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Cached recipes and analytics logs are periodically cleared. Users can delete all stored data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from device settings.

7. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access: Request access to your stored data.
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of local or cloud-stored data.
  • Opt-Out: Disable analytics or personalized recommendations in app settings.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: Stop data collection by uninstalling the app.

To exercise these rights, contact us at dev.ansari.mohiuddin@gmail.com.

Your data may be processed in countries other than your residence, depending on the APIs involved. We ensure appropriate safeguards consistent with this Privacy Policy.
